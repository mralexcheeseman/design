# PreviewPanel

The drawer the AnnouncementBar opens: the one full statement of what is real and what is a preview.

- The consumer provides: the heading ("About this preview") and its paragraphs.
- Same drawer as BagDrawer. Paragraphs are `body` in `ink-2`, at most four.
- Covers: the shirts are design visualisations; the hotels are fictional and this is not official merchandise; bag and saved items stay in the browser; what happens to an email address.
- This is the only long disclaimer on the site. Everywhere else gets at most one line.
