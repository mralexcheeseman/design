# Footer

The `staff-green` frame at the foot of the page: the newsletter, links and the wordmark.

- The consumer provides: the newsletter label and title, the SignupForm, link groups, the location and the small print.
- Text in `on-dark`; small print in `on-dark-2`. Links underline on hover; never `accent` on green.
- The Wordmark appears here once, with `on-dark`.
- Small print is `label` size or larger. Nothing in the footer drops below 12px on phones.
- `staff-green` appears here and in the AnnouncementBar only.
