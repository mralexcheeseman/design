# Hero

The first screen of the home page: one photograph of one shirt being worn, with the brand's one headline.

- The consumer provides: the photo, its alt text, a `label`, the two-line headline (the second line may be italic), one sentence and one Button.
- Full-bleed, the viewport minus the header (560px min, 920px max). Text sits bottom-left over a soft `ink` shade, in `on-dark`.
- On phones the photo is cropped with `mobileFocus` (84% 50% for the lifeguard) so the job title on the back reads in full.
- The headline is `display` (56px on phones). This is the only place the italic second line is allowed.
- One product, large. Never a collage of cut-outs, a carousel, or text over the shirt's print.
