# ProductCard

One shirt in the grid: its back on `stone` in a 4:5 frame, the front fading in on hover, then the name, the price and the hotel.

- The consumer provides: the back image (main), the front image (hover), the role name, the price in whole pounds, and "Hotel · Place".
- Images must sit on exactly `stone`, so the frame has no visible edge.
- Use three cards per row on desktop and two on phones. A lone last card keeps a half-width column; never stretch it.
- No badges, no quick-add, no back/front toggle and no heart. The whole card is one link to the product.
