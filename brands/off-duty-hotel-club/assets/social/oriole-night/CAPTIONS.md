# One night at The Oriole Palace (Hat Check carousel draft)

Seven frames, one hotel, one night. Every photo is taken from outside something: gate bars, bougainvillea, a door left ajar, a gap in the hedge, a shutter. The viewer is never invited in, until Security notices them.

1. The list: "7.42 pm. The gates are open. Your name is not on the list."
2. The keys: "Her car will be brought round. Eventually."
3. The door: "Someone left the door open. Just a crack."
4. The pool: "Nobody swims after dark. Nobody has to."
5. The bungalow: "Suite eleven. The pearls go on last."
6. Spotted: "Good evening. Can I help you?"
7. After: "6.10 am. One glove, a full key board, no explanation."

Post caption: "One night at The Oriole Palace. Security and Valet caps, from the Hat Check collection. Not on sale yet."

Draft only; nothing posted.
