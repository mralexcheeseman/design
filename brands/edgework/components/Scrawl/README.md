# Scrawl

A red-pen margin note in Rock Salt (`scrawl`), rotated -3° to -7°, in `red` (or `red-night` on night).

**Consumer provides:** the note, at most five words, lower case, with its own punctuation ("read that twice.").

**Rules:** one per card or section at most, and always beside the thing it comments on. A pen mark (arrow, circle or loop) may point at that thing.

**Never:** put a scrawl in a button, a heading or the masthead, or write more than one line.
