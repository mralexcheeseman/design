# Merch

What we would ask a supplier to make. Nothing here is ordered: samples need Alex's approval.

## The straps

| | Crossbody strap | Wrist strap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Webbing | 38 mm woven jacquard (polyester or cotton-poly), pattern woven in, not printed | Same webbing |
| Length | Adjustable 70–130 cm | 26 cm finished loop |
| Ends | Leather tabs in the destination's leather colour, stitched | One leather tab joining both ends |
| Hardware | Two gold-tone lobster clasps, one rectangular slider, zinc alloy, nickel-free plating | One gold-tone lobster clasp |
| Tether | Clear TPU tether card with a gold eyelet, fits under any phone case | Same |
| Price | £68 | £38 |

Leather colours: Amalfi cobalt, Marrakech terracotta, Kyoto deep indigo, Mexico City bougainvillea pink.

## Packaging

A paper envelope in `paper`, sealed with a sticker of the destination's postmark (ring version, 40 mm, one ink). No plastic.

## Samples

Ask two suppliers for one crossbody strap each, in Amalfi (the busiest pattern, so the hardest to weave cleanly). Score weave sharpness, colour match against the destination inks, leather edge finish, clasp weight and tether-card fit. Patterns go to the weaver as flat repeat artwork, which is still to be drawn: the renders are not artwork.
