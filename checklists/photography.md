# Photography review

- [ ] 4:5 portrait (`ratio-product`), product centred and filling about 78% of the width.
- [ ] The background is exactly the brand's `stone`, so the image sits on the page with no visible edge. Check the corners at 100%.
- [ ] Two images per product at least. For ODHC the back is the main image and the front the second.
- [ ] Clean studio photos were reframed in studio mode, never cut out.
- [ ] The hero is one product, large. On phones the crop keeps the key detail whole (for ODHC the job title is never cut mid-word).
- [ ] No text or captions on photos except the hero headline. No stock photography.
- [ ] Generated or mocked-up images are labelled as previews while `preview: true`.
